title: "Structural deficiency with respect to a directed hypergraph: information-theoretic constraints for generalized causal models"
venue: MIT CSAIL Theory Lunch
date: 2026-03-27
abstract: |
    What qualitative properties of a probability distribution can a graph express? The answer depends surprisingly on the formalism: arcs encode dependence in causal models, but independence in Bayesian networks. These notions interact, and both are special cases of something more general. We introduce a compatibility notion for directed hypergraphs, which are the qualitative backbone of probabilistic dependency graphs. This framework handles incomplete models, functional dependencies, and cyclic models, clarifying longstanding puzzles about multivariate mutual information in cyclic systems in the process. Based on [this paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.15488) with Spencer Peters and Joe Halpern.
